Frequently Asked Questions

Is this app safe for toddlers?

Yes! Dance Fruit Dance is completely ad-free with no external links or in-app purchases. It's designed specifically for ages 1-4 and is COPPA-compliant.

What devices does it work on?

Dance Fruit Dance works on iPhone and iPad running iOS 14 or later.

How do I use the app?

Simply tap any fruit to make it dance, jump, and sing! Each fruit has its own unique animation and sound.

Is there a refund policy?

All purchases are handled through the App Store. For refunds, please contact Apple Support directly through your App Store account.

How can I report a bug or suggest a feature?

We'd love to hear from you! Email us at support@youremail.com with details.

Privacy

We take your child's privacy seriously. Dance Fruit Dance does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Read our full Privacy Policy.
